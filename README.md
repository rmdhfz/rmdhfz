### 👨‍💻 About Me

With **8+ years of experience** in the telecommunications & Internet Service Provider industry, I specialize in designing large-scale backend systems, hardening infrastructure security, and leading engineering teams end-to-end — from architecture to production.

- 🔭 Currently **IT Development Team Lead** at **PT Sinergi Inti Andalan Prima Tbk (Sinergy Networks)**, and **IT Development Manager** at **PT Trans Hybrid Communication**.
- ⚙️ Primary stack: **Go (Echo v4) · MySQL · sqlx · Clean Architecture**.
- 🔐 Hands-on with infrastructure security: WAF hardening, vulnerability monitoring, and the MITRE ATT&CK framework.
- 🌱 Continuously exploring cloud-native architecture, DevSecOps, and Kubernetes-based platform engineering.
- 👯 Mentor junior developers and build internal learning materials to grow engineering teams.
- 💬 Ask me about **Go, Microservices, System Architecture, Database Tuning, or Cyber Security**.
